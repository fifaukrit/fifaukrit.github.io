Privacy Policy - Fifa Mac Mirror

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Fifa Mac Mirror is used only by its owner. It accesses the owner's Google Drive solely to copy the owner's own files between the owner's Mac and the owner's Drive.

  • No data is collected from anyone else.
  • Google user data is not sold, shared, transferred to third parties, or used for advertising.
  • Access tokens are stored only on the owner's own computer.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
